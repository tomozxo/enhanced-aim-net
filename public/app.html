How R6 fills your screen

Out of the box these are estimates, not R6's real formula — so drills can feel faster or slower than in-game. Fix it once, permanently: in R6, line your crosshair up with a mark, drag your mouse across the pad until you've turned exactly one full 360° back to that mark, and measure the total distance you moved with a ruler. Enter that number and this optic becomes exact — including when you change sens, since it calibrates the formula rather than pinning one value.
